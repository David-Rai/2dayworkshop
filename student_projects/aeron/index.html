ABOUt games

Outdoor games are physical activities played in open spaces like fields, parks, or yards. Popular examples include cricket, football (soccer), basketball, volleyball, and badminton. They help build strong bodies, teach teamwork, and keep the mind fresh.

football

Football (known as soccer in some countries) is the most popular sport in the world. Two teams of 11 players try to score by kicking a round ball into the other team's net. The game lasts 90 minutes, and players cannot use their hands except for the goalkeeper.
